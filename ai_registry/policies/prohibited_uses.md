# Prohibited Uses

Do not use Olympus for autonomous consequential decisions, legal or medical diagnosis, employment
screening, eligibility or credit decisions, biometric identification, covert surveillance, social
scoring, deception, harassment, unlawful discrimination, or generation of harmful instructions.

Do not enter secrets, credentials, special-category personal data, production customer data, or
unapproved confidential material. Do not bypass citations, human approval gates, access controls,
retention rules, or evidence compatibility checks.
