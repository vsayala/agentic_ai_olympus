# Acceptable Use

Olympus supports evidence-grounded document research, comparison, summarization, and controlled
evaluation. Users must verify material outputs against cited sources and apply human judgment before
acting on financial, legal, regulatory, employment, safety, or customer-impacting information.

Only approved corpora, models, environments, identities, and deployment paths may be used. Report
suspected data exposure, unsafe output, citation failure, or control bypass through the approved
incident process.
