# Risk Appetite

Olympus accepts low residual risk for internal, reversible, evidence-grounded assistance. It has
zero appetite for secret leakage, unauthorized data egress, hidden tool execution, unsupported
claims presented as evidence, autonomous consequential decisions, or production deployment without
human approval.

Release is blocked by any critical or high security finding, stale registry review, missing
specialist receipt, failed citation or permission boundary, unresolved material bias finding, or
unapproved governance exception. Medium findings require a named owner and dated remediation;
accepted exceptions require human approval and expiry.
