# Retention Schedule

Repository manifests and policy history follow Git retention. Governance receipts, release
attestations, incidents, exceptions, deployment approvals, and operational logs follow the approved
organizational schedule in their system of record. Source documents follow their data owner's
schedule and must not be copied into governance evidence.

Before production deployment, the human governance owner must record concrete retention periods,
legal holds, deletion verification, and system-of-record locations. Where no approved period exists,
collection is blocked rather than retained indefinitely.
