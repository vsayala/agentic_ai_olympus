# Registry Ownership

- The human governance owner is accountable for classification, approval, exceptions, and review
  dates.
- Odin coordinates registry validation and integration; Odin does not approve policy changes.
- Loki reviews data categories, source lineage, retention, retrieval, monitoring, and Databricks.
- Thor reviews system components, models, vendors, prompts, tools, permissions, and guardrails.
- Hela reviews human oversight, notices, accessibility, interaction, and user-visible failures.
- CODEOWNERS approval is required for every registry change.

Named organizational and legal owners must replace role placeholders before production use outside
the current repository-controlled pilot.
