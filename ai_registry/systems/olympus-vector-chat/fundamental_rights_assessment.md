# Fundamental Rights Assessment: Olympus Vector Chat

**Status:** Preliminary applicability screening; human legal/governance confirmation required.

The approved use is internal assistive document research. Autonomous consequential decisions,
employment or eligibility decisions, biometric uses, surveillance, social scoring, and unlawful
discrimination are prohibited. On that scope, a formal FRIA is not currently asserted as complete
or applicable.

Reassess before any people-impacting, public-sector, essential-service, employment, credit,
education, law-enforcement, migration, justice, or other high-risk deployment.
